Methodology - Stress Test Model

Calculates the Sustainability Horizon (τ) based on the economic impact of rising resource costs.

1. Extra Annual Cost (ΔC):
ΔCi(t) = Σ [ui,r × (cr(t) - cr(b))]
2. Projected Profit (P):
Pi(t) = Pi - ΔCi(t)
3. Sustainability Horizon (τ):
τi = min(t - b)
where ΔCi(t) ≥ Pi

The calculation is made for each resource separately. 140+ means the company does not reach zero profit within the available 2026–2100 projections.

The sliders add annual growth g to a resource's cost, so the cost at year t carries a factor (1+g)t and the crossing moves earlier. The price path cr(t) is recovered from the per-resource columns of the table itself: a company's single-resource horizon fixes the price rise at that year as Pi/ui,r. At g = 0 the figure is the table's own.
