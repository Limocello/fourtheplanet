Circular Steel
Sustainability Documents
Sustainability is foundational to Nucor, from responsibly sourcing scrap metal to melting and manufacturing it into new
steel products. Our electric arc furnace steelmaking operations are designed to support the circular economy. By
prioritizing recycled content and continuous emissions reduction, we lower the embodied carbon of the steel used to build
essential infrastructure. These practices enable long-term environmental responsibility while delivering high-quality,
domestically produced steel for a more sustainable future.
